# peace by satomi
## purpose

this is a quiet record of how i think.

it is not a portfolio, a personal brand, or a place to present a finished version of myself.

i want to preserve what i think, what i question, what i notice, and how my thoughts change over time.

peace is the direction of my thought. it does not need to be a definition or a conclusion.



## writing

write in lowercase by default.

use capitals only when genuinely necessary.

use yyyymmdd for dates.

be quiet, honest, and thoughtful.

do not force a conclusion.

uncertainty is allowed.

contradiction is allowed.

changing my mind is allowed.

unfinished thoughts are allowed.

do not make the writing sound more inspirational or certain than it really is.

preserve the feeling of a real person thinking.


## visual direction

minimal

quiet

simple

lots of empty space

mostly monochrome

no unnecessary decoration

no visual noise

the initial font is space grotesk.

the font should be defined as a css variable so it can be changed later without changing the content.


## things to avoid

advertisements

visitor counters

likes or follower counts

popups

autoplay

unnecessary notifications

marketing language

exaggerated claims

unnecessary animations

anything that makes the site feel like a commercial product


## content

possible subjects include peace, philosophy, perception, martial art (kendo) , food, science, data, immigration, motherhood, grief, anger, books, nature, and everyday observations.

these subjects do not need to be separated into strict categories.

the deeper question is how they connect to the way i understand people, life, and peace.


## preserving change

old thoughts should not disappear simply because my thinking changes.

the history of the project is part of the project.

git history can preserve how an idea changed over time.

the goal is not to show that i was always right.

the goal is to leave a record of how i thought.
